THE ENGINES

One companion. A whole engine room.

Every part of the system is its own engine with one job, watched by the others. Nothing runs on vibes — it runs on heartbeats, ledgers and seals.

🎙️

Voice Engine

Alpha's ears and voice. It hears you, thinks, and answers out loud — with barge-in, so you can cut in mid-sentence like a real conversation. A voice-key gate means the engine wakes for its owner’s voice, and no one else’s.

LISTEN · THINK · SPEAK
💓

Engine Core

The beating heart at the centre. A signed, hash-chained soul ledger ticks every five seconds — every beat links to the one before it, so the engine’s whole life is one unbroken, provable chain.

BEAT EVERY 5s · CHAIN VERIFIED
🛡️

MadShield

The layered guard. It seals the system’s important files into a baseline and checks them in layers — if anything drifts from the seal, the guard says so, plainly, and never quietly patches over it.

SEALED BASELINE · LAYERED CHECKS
🧱

MadWall

The wall watcher. It keeps a signed ledger of everything that listens or reaches out from the machine, and flags any touch it has not seen before. Observe, record, report — never react on its own.

SIGNED WALL LEDGER
🧅

Tor Privacy Lane

A private lane for private work. Traffic that needs discretion rides through Tor with bridge relays, and the lane proves itself — a through-Tor check must come back verified before anything trusts it.

VERIFIED PRIVATE EGRESS
🧠

Hybrid Brain

The memory muscle. Tens of thousands of notes, builds and lessons indexed and searchable in milliseconds, so Alpha answers from the record — not from a guess — and every build makes the next one smarter.

55,000+ INDEXED CHUNKS
🐺

How they work together

The core beats, the wall watches, the shield seals, the voice speaks — and a self-heal routine quietly puts anything back on its feet when the ground moves. Each engine is small and honest about its one job; the strength is in how they check each other.
